# GOJIAS COLLECTIONS User Personas
## Persona 1: Customer

### Name
Grace Achieng

### Age
28

### Occupation
Marketing Officer

### Location
Kisumu, Kenya

### Background
Grace is a working professional who enjoys wearing custom-made African-inspired clothing for work, weddings, church, and social events. She values convenience and prefers ordering services online because of her busy schedule.

### Goals
- Find trusted dressmakers near her location.
- Order custom-made clothing without visiting multiple tailoring shops.
- Buy ready-made African-inspired outfits online.
- Receive quality garments that fit well.
- Track the progress of her orders.

### Frustrations
- Difficulty finding reliable dressmakers.
- Poor communication during the tailoring process.
- Delayed order completion.
- Clothes that do not fit correctly.

### Technology Usage
- Uses a smartphone daily.
- Shops online regularly.
- Uses M-Pesa for most payments.

### Quote
"I want beautiful clothes made by trusted dressmakers without the stress of moving from one shop to another."

## Persona 2: Dressmaker

### Name
Mary Atieno

### Age
35

### Occupation
Professional Dressmaker and Fashion Designer

### Location
Kisumu, Kenya

### Background
Mary owns a small tailoring business specializing in African-inspired clothing. She has built a loyal customer base through referrals but wants to expand her business by reaching more customers online. She is passionate about creating high-quality garments and growing her brand.

### Goals
- Promote her tailoring business to a wider audience.
- Showcase her portfolio to attract new customers.
- Receive custom clothing orders online.
- Sell ready-made clothing through the platform.
- Manage customer appointments and orders efficiently.
- Build a strong reputation through positive customer reviews.

### Frustrations
- Limited visibility beyond her local community.
- Irregular customer flow.
- Difficulty managing multiple customer orders using phone calls and messaging apps.
- Customers providing incomplete or inaccurate measurements.
- Late payments or cancelled orders.

### Technology Usage
- Uses a smartphone and a laptop for business.
- Communicates with customers through WhatsApp and phone calls.
- Uses M-Pesa and bank transfers for payments.
- Regularly posts her work on social media to attract customers.

### Quote
"I want more people to discover my work and trust my business without depending only on word-of-mouth referrals."

## Persona 3: Administrator

### Name
Daniel Otieno

### Age
32

### Occupation
Platform Administrator

### Location
Kisumu, Kenya

### Background
Daniel is responsible for managing the daily operations of GOJIAS COLLECTIONS. He ensures that dressmakers meet the platform's quality standards, monitors customer activity, resolves disputes, oversees transactions, and maintains the overall integrity of the platform.

### Goals
- Verify and approve qualified dressmakers.
- Ensure a safe and trustworthy marketplace.
- Monitor platform activity and user accounts.
- Resolve customer and dressmaker disputes efficiently.
- Generate reports to evaluate platform performance.
- Maintain high customer satisfaction.

### Frustrations
- Fraudulent or incomplete dressmaker applications.
- Customer complaints and order disputes.
- Fake reviews or inappropriate content.
- Failed or delayed payment transactions.
- Managing a growing number of users and orders.

### Technology Usage
- Uses a desktop computer for daily platform management.
- Monitors dashboards and reports.
- Uses email and the administration portal to communicate with users.
- Reviews platform analytics to support decision-making.

### Quote
"My goal is to ensure that every customer has a safe, reliable, and satisfying experience while helping genuine dressmakers grow their businesses."
