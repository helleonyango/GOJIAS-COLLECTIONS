# GOJIAS COLLECTIONS User Journey
## 1. Customer Journey

### Goal
To successfully order a custom-made or ready-made outfit.

### Journey

1. Visit the GOJIAS COLLECTIONS website.
2. Create an account or log in.
3. Browse ready-made clothing or search for dressmakers by location.
4. View dressmaker profiles, portfolios, ratings, and reviews.
5. Select a dressmaker or ready-made clothing item.
6. If ordering custom clothing:
   - Upload a measurement sheet.
   - Book a fitting appointment if necessary.
   - Provide design preferences and special instructions.
7. Review the order details.
8. Choose a payment method:
   - M-Pesa
   - Cash on Delivery
   - Bank Transfer
9. Confirm the order.
10. Receive order confirmation.
11. Track order progress through the platform.
12. Receive the completed garment.
13. Rate and review the dressmaker and overall experience.

## 2. Dressmaker Journey

### Goal
To receive and manage customer orders while growing a successful tailoring business through GOJIAS COLLECTIONS.

### Journey

1. Visit the GOJIAS COLLECTIONS website.
2. Create a dressmaker account.
3. Complete the profile by providing:
   - Business information
   - Location
   - Contact details
   - Years of experience
   - Portfolio images
4. Submit the profile for administrator verification.
5. Receive approval from the administrator.
6. Publish the profile and portfolio.
7. Add ready-made clothing listings.
8. Receive notifications of new customer orders.
9. Accept or decline customer orders.
10. Review customer measurements and design preferences.
11. Schedule or confirm fitting appointments when necessary.
12. Begin creating the garment.
13. Update the order status throughout production.
14. Confirm payment status.
15. Mark the order as completed.
16. Receive customer ratings and reviews.
17. Monitor business performance through the dashboard.
## 3. Administrator Journey

### Goal
To maintain a secure, trustworthy, and efficient marketplace by managing users, monitoring platform activities, and ensuring smooth platform operations.

### Journey

1. Log in to the administrator dashboard.
2. View the dashboard overview, including:
   - Total customers
   - Total dressmakers
   - Pending dressmaker applications
   - Active orders
   - Recent platform activity
3. Review new dressmaker applications.
4. Approve, request changes, or reject dressmaker applications.
5. Monitor customer and dressmaker accounts.
6. Review and manage product listings.
7. Monitor customer orders and payment transactions.
8. Respond to customer and dressmaker support requests.
9. Review reported issues, disputes, and inappropriate content.
10. Moderate customer ratings and reviews when necessary.
11. Publish announcements and promotional messages.
12. Generate reports and analyze platform performance.
13. Update platform settings and configurations when required.
14. Log out securely.
