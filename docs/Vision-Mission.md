# GOJIAS COLLECTIONS

## Vision
To become the leading digital platform that connects customers with skilled dressmakers across Africa and beyond, making custom-made and ready-made fashion more accessible, trusted, and convenient.

## Mission
To empower customers and dressmakers by providing a secure, user-friendly digital platform where customers can discover skilled dressmakers, order custom-made or ready-made clothing, schedule measurements and appointments, make secure payments, and receive high-quality fashion services while supporting the growth of local tailoring businesses

## Core Values

### 1. Customer Satisfaction

We are committed to providing customers with a reliable, convenient, and enjoyable experience by delivering high-quality tailoring services and products that meet or exceed expectations.

### 2. Quality Craftsmanship

We value excellence in tailoring and encourage dressmakers to produce garments that demonstrate skill, creativity, and attention to detail.

### 3. Trust and Transparency

We promote honesty, secure transactions, fair pricing, authentic reviews, and clear communication between customers, dressmakers, and administrators.

### 4. Empowerment of Local Artisans

We support talented dressmakers by providing them with a digital platform to showcase their work, grow their businesses, and reach more customers.

### 5. Innovation

We embrace technology to simplify custom tailoring through online ordering, digital measurements, appointment scheduling, secure payments, and customer notifications.

### 6. Inclusivity

We welcome customers and dressmakers from diverse backgrounds and aim to make the platform accessible across different regions and communities.

### 7. Professionalism

We encourage respectful communication, ethical business practices, accountability, and continuous improvement among all platform users.

## Business Goals

### Short-Term Goals (Version 1)

- Successfully launch the GOJIAS COLLECTIONS website.
- Enable customers to order both custom-made and ready-made clothing online.
- Onboard and verify skilled dressmakers.
- Provide secure payment options, including M-Pesa, Bank Transfer, and Cash on Delivery.
- Establish a trusted review and rating system.
- Build a strong reputation for quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

### Medium-Term Goals (1–3 Years)

- Expand the dressmaker network across multiple counties in Kenya.
- Launch the GOJIAS COLLECTIONS mobile application.
- Introduce delivery partnerships for faster and more reliable order fulfillment.
- Increase customer retention through loyalty programs and personalized recommendations.

### Long-Term Goals (3–5 Years)

- Expand operations to multiple African countries.
- Become the leading online marketplace for African fashion and custom tailoring.
- Create employment opportunities by supporting thousands of local dressmakers.
- Build strategic partnerships with fashion designers, textile suppliers, and training institutions.

## Long-Term Objectives

1. Build the most trusted online marketplace for custom-made and ready-made African fashion.

2. Empower thousands of dressmakers and fashion entrepreneurs by providing them with digital tools to grow their businesses.

3. Preserve and promote African fashion, including Ankara, Kitenge, Kitenge-inspired, and other traditional designs, by connecting them with customers across Africa and internationally.

4. Expand GOJIAS COLLECTIONS into multiple African countries while maintaining high standards of quality, trust, and customer satisfaction.

5. Create employment opportunities by supporting local artisans, delivery partners, photographers, fashion designers, and other professionals within the fashion industry.

6. Continuously innovate by introducing new technologies and services that improve the customer and dressmaker experience.

7. Build strategic partnerships with fashion institutions, textile manufacturers, logistics providers, and payment service providers to strengthen the platform's ecosystem.
